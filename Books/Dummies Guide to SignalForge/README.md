# SignalForge | Book 01 — Dummies Guide to SignalForge

A plain-English, living book for curious readers with no prerequisite trading or software knowledge. The book explains trading rules, backtesting, scientific validation and AI-assisted engineering without implying trading profitability or real-money readiness.
