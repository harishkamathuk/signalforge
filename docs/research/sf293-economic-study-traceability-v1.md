# SF-293 — Prospective economic study requirements-to-evidence matrix v1

**Contract:** `sf293-economic-study-traceability-v1` — **template, not an approved study**.  
**Applicability:** each **new** protected economic research study, before economic Gate D.  
**Authority:** [00.01 owner decision #6885045585](https://github.com/harishkamathuk/signalforge/issues/253#issuecomment-6885045585), [economic-objective gate #6884817873](https://github.com/harishkamathuk/signalforge/issues/253#issuecomment-6884817873), [04 M1–M4 review #6884967612](https://github.com/harishkamathuk/signalforge/issues/293#issuecomment-6884967612), [#293](https://github.com/harishkamathuk/signalforge/issues/293).  
**Gate checklist:** [prospective economic Gate D](../engineering/prospective-economic-gate-d-checklist.md).

This is a **normative contract/template**, not completed evidence, an execution permit or a substitute for an individually frozen preregistration. For every future study, instantiate **one versioned, study-specific copy** under `docs/research/`, fill all fields, freeze the approved revision before protected Gate D, and obtain independent 04 ACCEPT and explicit 00.01 GO. **An uninstantiated template, a blank cell, a component-only CI PASS or a historical study's PASS cannot admit an economic study.** Do not select, relax or backfill criteria after results are observed.

The matrix links a predeclared *research answer* to its exact operable and independently testable evidence path. It is not a new execution orchestrator or software interface.

**Cost policy (M1):** `signalforge.research.scenario_reportability` currently supports **scenario-conditioned gross modeled R**, not validated cost/funding-adjusted net profitability. A study asking about after-cost benefit is **economic Gate D NO-GO** until separately approved assumptions and appropriately tested cost-aware reporting exist. A narrower gross-R-only study requires explicit, prior 00.01 approval, documented scientific utility, thresholds and prohibition on inferred net/realised profits. Missing economics must **never** be filled with fictional funding, fees or margin estimates.

**Population/decision policy (M2):** conditional CLOSED-contained expectancy/drawdown are statistics of the included modeled cohort, **not unconditional strategy expectancy**. Explicitly reconcile signalled and actionable opportunities, included CLOSED, excluded CROSS_WINDOW, OPEN/ARMED right-censored, REJECTED, EXPIRED, and independently known strict eligible/assessed/ambiguous/unassessed/indeterminate one-minute populations in each window. Define the *numeric* sample threshold and maximum tolerable exclusions/unknowns **before exposure**; breached limits produce preregistered INCONCLUSIVE/WITHHELD, never a retrospectively selected flattering sample. A complete known-zero eligible strict population is distinct from not-assessed/indeterminate evidence. Strict source-derived E/I/? cannot be inferred from modeled O→H→L→C.

## Required row schema and admission discipline

Each instantiated ER row **must include**: requirement ID; specific economic/scientific deliverable and rationale; normative authority URL/path; named 02/04/00.01 owner; prerequisite; **exact production-intended guarded launcher/worker → producer/writer → receipt → saved-byte verifier → authorised output** path with revision and version; frozen input/manifest/source and artifact hashes; synthetic positive, independent numeric-oracle and adversarial test references including exact CI SHA; phase (`PRE_GATE_D` or `POSTRUN_ONLY`); evidence disposition (`PROVEN`, `MISSING`, `CONDITIONAL`, `POSTRUN_ONLY`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`); independent reviewer, owner decision and stop/withhold consequence. **Every mandatory PRE_GATE_D item must be PROVEN for the explicitly approved economic question.** `CONDITIONAL` is not an economic admission loophole: a criterion may be out of scope only when the prospective owner-approved **different economic question** makes it genuinely nonmandatory. Unknown or absent mandatory proof ⇒ **economic NO-GO**. `POSTRUN_ONLY` must not be mislabeled PROVEN by synthetic work.

## Actual-path synthetic acceptance — demonstrable pre-D, not historical evidence (M3/M4)

An approved prospective test must invoke the **same guarded operator entry point** and writer/producer/receipt path planned for protected execution; a test that merely imports and calls individual components **does not count**. With conspicuously artificial source bars and isolated private custody, prove:

1. **Positive capability:** independently derived complete synthetic source-quality and strict companions; persisted canonical v2 bound by exact source/strict SHA and run/result identity, verified after deleting/discarding retained runner object; externally authored known positive and **negative** gross-R economic oracles; empty/zero/undefined and CROSS_WINDOW/right-censored cases with explicit denominators and exclusions. Validate cost-adjusted scenario as required by the prospective approved question—not by claiming existing SF-283 provides it.
2. **Independent strict fail-closed:** source-invalid/unknown, true independent strict early-termination, E/I/? suffix, eligible-unassessed/ambiguous and rehashed contradictory companion produce exact expected WITHHELD/INCONCLUSIVE states. A simulated all-zero eligible dataset may count as complete only when the independent strict run establishes full traversed ineligibility.
3. **Security/nonleak:** protected-result/default OOS access always denied; no OOS economics, OOS–IS profile differences, trace arrays, raw private values or partial economic metrics in *any* stdout, stderr, log, failure, receipt or accessible synthetic IS-first report (even where v2 sealed private output internally holds both windows). Missing, forged, wrong or stale owner authority fails before disclosure.
4. **Custody/adversarial:** v1 fallback, unbound/missing/swapped/corrupt source or strict, inconsistent scenario identity/schema/window/RAW profile, wrong costs, crash, timeout, OOM, partial result and overwrite/collision all stop or withhold without marking study qualified. Source/strategy/ADR semantics never mutate.

The independent oracle must not be computed from the report under test. Preserve raw synthetic SHA, exact review head, CI result and sanitized allowlisted outputs in the per-study matrix. Protect proprietary and private historical data: **synthetic proof is capability only**.

## Postrun gates, ownership and non-goals

**00.01** owns question, thresholds/cost scope, Gate D, any distinct OOS release and Gate E decisions. **04** owns research preregistration, independent B1–B5/strict/cohort scrutiny and report acceptance. **02** owns exact code/launcher/reader/writer/release/identity integration and test provenance; operator owns private custody and permitted invocation.

Historical B3 strict coverage, actual runner peak RSS, historical canonical repeats, observed source/data limitations and true study outcomes cannot be established by preregistration or synthetic tests. They are **POSTRUN_ONLY** and require later authorised protected evidence and decisions. Adverse/negative modeled outcomes may be scientifically valid; an unanswerable economic question is a failed research deliverable. Preserve `SCENARIO_CONDITIONED`, `execution_valid=false`, `UNQUALIFIED_HISTORICAL_METADATA` and any `REPEATED_SAMPLE` qualification when applicable.

**Frozen SF-260 v1 is unaffected.** This file does not approve a new launcher, v2 integration, cost-model code, protected source use, real history, result inspection, OOS release, Gate E or paper/live.
