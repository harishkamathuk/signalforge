**Scope decision:** A statement of *economic benefit, profitability or investment usefulness after costs* defaults to **cost-adjusted hypothetical** evidence. A narrower scenario-conditioned **gross modeled R** question is allowable only if 00.01 **prospectively** approves that different question and its thresholds/limitations. A feasibility-only exercise is a separate, prospectively authorised **NON-ECONOMIC** study, not an economic Gate D waiver. Never invent cost inputs, label model outputs as actual fills or equate gross R with net profits.

**Time boundary:** G1–G7 prove the *capacity to produce and safely qualify the planned answer* using synthetic evidence. They **do not** prove real historical B3 strict eligibility, actual worker RSS, historical source/replay integrity, results or economic value. These remain `POSTRUN_ONLY`, assessed under subsequent 04 research validation, 00.01 IS/OOS decisions and Gate E. A future actual strict producer may legitimately yield indeterminate/incomplete source evidence; reporting then **WITHHELD** is correct, not permission to relax gates.

**Owner and status:** 02 binds implementation, artifact and custody integration; 04 owns preregistration, scientific sufficiency and independent acceptance; 00.01 owns economics/cost scope, Gate D and subsequent OOS/Gate E decisions. An uninstantiated matrix template, **MISSING** mandatory proof or absent owner GO is a **hard economic NO-GO**. Every exception requires an explicitly different, owner-approved prospective question or NON-ECONOMIC classification; no retrospective downgrade.

This document is **normative admission guidance only**. No runtime implementation, new protected Gate D, SF-260 v1 access/reclassification, historical execution, v1→v2 conversion, OOS release or live deployment is authorised.
